# SkillTwin video recording guidance and analysis limits

This document provides clear recording instructions and analysis limits for supervisors capturing packaging demonstrations. These guidelines inform the upload page design and help ensure reliable video observation.

## Recommended recording practices

### 1. Camera positioning and framing

- Mount camera at an overhead angle or high 45 degree angle looking down at the packaging bench
- Keep the entire work area in frame, including incoming product, carton, tape dispenser, and label rolls
- Ensure worker hands and packaging materials remain unobstructed throughout the demonstration
- Avoid standing directly between the camera lens and the packaging bench

### 2. Lighting and environment

- Use bright diffuse overhead lighting to minimize deep shadows
- Avoid backlighting from open windows or bright overhead lamps directly behind the subject
- Maintain a clean and tidy bench surface with unnecessary tools removed

### 3. Video duration and resolution

- Recommended video duration is between 15 and 90 seconds per demonstration
- The system enforces a hard ceiling of 180 seconds per video
- Upload standard landscape orientation video at 1080p or 720p resolution at 25 or 30 frames per second
- Accepted formats are MP4, WebM, and QuickTime MOV

### 4. Spoken narration guidance

- Speak clearly during the demonstration to describe major packaging transitions
- Good examples of spoken cues:
  - Inspect the ceramic mug for surface cracks
  - Assemble the standard corrugated box
  - Wrap the mug with bubble wrap
  - Lower the wrapped product into the center
  - Seal the box using H tape method
  - Attach the fragile shipping label on top
- Narration is optional. Silent videos are fully supported and will record null spoken evidence

## Analysis limits and boundaries

1. Six step workflow template:
   - Media intelligence classifies actions strictly into the six standard steps: selectProduct, selectBox, addProtection, placeProduct, sealBox, attachLabel
   - The system does not invent unseen actions to complete the template

2. Visual resolution limits:
   - The model observes visible macroscopic actions and object presence
   - The system cannot verify microscopic hairline cracks or chemical adhesive properties

3. Worker privacy:
   - The model analyzes physical objects and bench actions only
   - Worker faces and personal identities are not tracked or stored in observation records
